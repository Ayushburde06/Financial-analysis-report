## PAGE 1 LAYOUT — Two-column design

### LEFT COLUMN (main content, ~65% width)

**Zone 1 — Company Header (top-left)**
- www.geojit.com logo top-left
- Company name: LARGE BOLD (e.g. "Eternal Ltd.")
- Report type: "Q1FY26 Result Update"
- No sector line here — sector is in the sidebar

**Zone 2 — Business Description (1 paragraph, NO heading)**
- 2-3 sentences describing what the company does
- Written in third person, factual
- Example: "Eternal Limited, formerly Zomato Limited, operates as an online food delivery company. It runs a B2C platform under the Zomato brand. The company also operates Hyperpure and Blinkit, organises events, provides payment services and engages in investment activities."

**Zone 3 — Key Highlights (bullet list, NO heading label needed)**
- 5-6 bullet points using • symbol
- Each bullet = ONE crisp sentence with a specific number
- Format: "[Metric] [verb] [YoY/QoQ %] to Rs. [value] in [period]"
- Examples:
  • Consolidated revenue from operations surged 70.4% YoY in Q1FY26 to Rs. 7,167cr as all segments demonstrated robust growth.
  • EBITDA fell 35.0% YoY to Rs. 115cr in Q1FY26, mainly due to higher operating expenses; consequently EBITDA margin declined 260bps to 1.6%.
  • Reported PAT plunged 90.1% YoY to Rs. 25cr in Q1FY26 because of lower EBITDA.

**Zone 4 — Report Subtitle (italic, below bullets)**
- One-line investment thesis: "Blinkit propels growth; valuation limits upside"
- Format: [key driver]; [key concern]

**Zone 5 — Outlook & Valuation (headed section, bottom of main column)**
- Heading: "Outlook & Valuation"
- 4-5 sentences as a paragraph (NO bullets)
- Structure:
  1. Company's position / long-term outlook
  2. Growth drivers / strategy
  3. Risk / competitive concern
  4. Recommendation sentence with target price
- Example last sentence: "Therefore, we downgrade our rating on the stock to HOLD from BUY with a revised target price of Rs. 337, based on 6x FY27 price/sales."
- If CMP/Target not available: "Valuation data not available in source document. We withhold a price target."

**Zone 6 — Quarterly Financials Table (bottom of page 1 main)**
- Title: "Quarterly Financials Consolidated"
- Columns: Rs.cr | [Current Quarter] | [YoY Quarter] | YoY Growth (%) | [QoQ Quarter] | QoQ Growth (%)
- Rows: Sales, EBITDA, Margin (%), EBIT, PBT, Rep. PAT, Adj PAT, Adj. EPS (Rs)
- Growth shown as number (e.g. 70.4) NOT as "+70.4%"
- Missing values: show — (em dash)

## NARRATIVE RULES (for AI-generated sections)

1. Business Description: factual, 2-3 sentences, third person, NO financial numbers
2. Key Highlights bullets: ALWAYS include a number + context (YoY/QoQ %)
3. Outlook & Valuation: analytical tone, ends with recommendation sentence
4. If data not available: write "Not available in source document" — NEVER invent
5. Use "Rs." not "₹" symbol (avoids PDF encoding issues)
6. Use "cr" for crores, "bn" for billions
7. Growth figures: write as "+15.8% YoY" or "-3.2% QoQ"
8. No markdown bold (**text**) in output
9. No [Source:] citation tags in final PDF text
10. No section headings starting with "##" — use clean plain headings
